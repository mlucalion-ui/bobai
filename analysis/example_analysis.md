## 2. Voice and tone

- **First-person plural throughout** — "we", "our", "Scotch are…" (treats Scotch as a plural collective, British construction-industry convention).
- **Warm-but-professional opener** — the introduction begins with a stock "delighted to have been invited by [named client] of [org]" line. Mild informality permitted ("a great project", "hit the ground running").
- **British English** — programme, organisation, etc.
- **Confident, not boastful** — claims are quantified (c.65 people, c.97% retention, £10M PI cover) rather than rhetorical.
- **Technical abbreviations used without expansion once introduced** — RIBA, MEP, EPC, BREEAM, AP, ERs, CPs, URS, BSRIA, AHU, PCSA, LZC.
- **Sentence length** — mostly medium, factual. Bullets carry technical detail; prose carries positioning.
- **Bullet conventions** — top-level `•`, nested `o`. Bullets are short clauses, not full sentences (no terminal punctuation).
- **Tables for fees, rates, insurance** — never prose where a table will do.
- **Minor surface roughness** — occasional inconsistent capitalisation ("Scotch partners"), spaced parens "( if required)". This is real-engineer prose, not marketing copy; I'll mirror, not over-polish.

## 3. Section content patterns

### 3.1 Introduction (~250 words)
- 1 opening sentence: "Scotch Partners are delighted to have been invited by [client name] of [org] to provide a fee and scope for the design of the [project name] [scope]."
- 1–2 sentences positioning the project and Scotch's role/history with it (e.g. "involved during the acquisition stage").
- 1 sentence acknowledging programme/urgency from the client side.
- A short bulleted summary of the scheme: site, scope, planning posture, EPC/energy treatment, programme milestones, what fees are for.
- No section-end statement — flows directly into Section 2.

### 3.2 Scotch Partners Introduction (~600 words, 5 sub-sections)
- **Lead paragraph**: 1–2 sentences linking Scotch's track record to the project's sector ("workplace and science and technology", "residential / co-living"), then a 1-sentence definition of Scotch ("a building design practice providing mechanical, electrical & public health engineering, sustainability, energy and planning (SEP) and acoustic consultancy services").
- **Management Structure** (~120 words): partner count, flat structure, what reporting/resource flexibility this enables.
- **Quality & Delivery** (~120 words): design philosophy, "test and validate", commitment to consistent staffing.
- **Financial Standing & Ownership** (1 sentence): wholly owned by Partners, no borrowings.
- **Our Clients**: a logo strip (image, not extracted) preceded by one lead-in sentence.
- **Team & Relationships** (~50 words): collaborative ethos, long-lasting relationships.

This whole section is almost entirely **reusable boilerplate**. Only the lead paragraph references the specific sector.

### 3.3 Team and Proposed Roles (~220 words)
- 3.1 Organogram (image).
- 1 paragraph: "We propose to use the same team members that supported the acquisition stage" — references team continuity and London (Farringdon) office.
- 1 paragraph: reporting/communication rationale.
- 1 paragraph: names the **core leadership team** with role per person ([Lead] as overall point of contact, supported by [X on utilities/electrical], [Y on sustainability], [Z on acoustics]).
- 1 paragraph: nominates a separate **MEP co-ordinator** for design-team liaison.

Pattern is heavily project-specific (named individuals tied to scope strands) but the *structure of the paragraph* — leadership trio + dedicated co-ordinator — is reusable.

### 3.4 Fees (~700 words across 3 pages)
- 1-sentence intro: "Our overall MEP and Energy fees can be summarised in the table below."
- **Table 1**: MEP fees + Energy modelling (EPC) by RIBA stage, with a Notes column, ending in a totals row and a grand total.
- 1-sentence intro: "Optional fees for Acoustics can be found below:-"
- **Table 2**: Noise Survey + Acoustics by stage.
- 1-sentence intro: "Optional fees for BREEAM can be found below:-"
- **Table 3**: BREEAM + BREEAM AP by stage.
- **Clarifications**: ~15 bulleted exclusions/assumptions. This is a recurring shape — VAT, expenses basis, programme assumption, planning assumptions, Stage 4 assumption, fire engineering excluded, energy methodology caveats, EPC lodging excluded, acoustic on-site testing excluded, vibration excluded, etc.
- **Time Charge Rates**: 6-row table by grade (Partner / Tech Director / Principal / Senior / Engineer / Asst-Graduate) with £/hour. A short caveat that these differ from the rates used to calculate the fee.
- **Insurance**: 3-row table (PI / EL / PL) with cover value, provider, expiry. Mentions PI £10M, EL £10M, PL £10M. Notes that copies of certificates are included in this section.

### 3.5 Appendix A — Scope of Services (~1500 words)
Scope is split by service strand, then by RIBA stage. Within each stage, content is a flat bulleted deliverables list.
- **MEP**: Stage 1 (already done), Stage 2 concept design (~15 bullets), Stage 3+ scheme design / tender (~15 bullets), Stage 4 i/ii/iii (client monitoring only — short paragraph), Stage 5 (sample review prose paragraph + bullets), Stage 6 (commissioning review bullets).
- **Energy**: Stage 2 + Stage 3 — short, 3–4 bullets each.
- **BREEAM**: Stage 2 / 3+ / 4 / 5–6 — 3–4 bullets each; followed by a small table of optional credit activities + 3 footnote bullets.
- **Acoustics**: RIBA Stage 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 & 6 — longer bullet lists, particularly Stage 3 (about 15 bullets).

This is the most cut-and-paste-able body of the document. Only Stage 1 / Stage 2 framing sentences carry specific project context ("This has been undertaken already as part of the acquisition stage…").

### 3.6 Appendix B — Terms & Conditions (~1000 words)
16 numbered clauses covering: skill/care, invoicing terms, late payment, PI insurance, liability cap (10× fee), net-contribution-style limit, indemnity over the cap, employee non-pursuit, IP/copyright, termination (insolvency), termination (default), termination payment, non-assignment / third-party rights, dispute resolution (CIC adjudication, English law), entire agreement, bespoke contracts £10k threshold. Verbatim legal boilerplate — should be reproduced unchanged.

## 4. Standard phrases and positioning (Scotch's reusable language)

These should be carried across roughly verbatim into the new response. Listed by section.

**Section 1 (Introduction) — stock opener pattern:**
- "Scotch Partners are delighted to have been invited by [Client lead] of [Client] to provide a fee and scope for the design of the [project] redevelopment."
- "This is a great project and we are delighted to have been involved during the acquisition stage of the scheme…"
- "…the design team can hit the ground running."

**Section 2 — capability statement:**
- "Scotch have a proven track record working with [sectors X and Y] clients and are currently working a number of similar [scheme type] schemes."
- "Scotch are a building design practice providing mechanical, electrical & public health engineering, sustainability, energy and planning (SEP) and acoustic consultancy services."
- "The culture of the practice is founded upon respect for people and trust."
- "…creating a great and inclusive working environment where the best minds in the industry will flourish."
- "…high retention rate which was c97% for recently completed FY 24."
- "Since its inception, Scotch has organically grown being careful to employ people that believe in our shared values of quality, collaboration, and trust."
- "With a team of circa 65 people, our size and ownership enable us to have total control over workload and resource…"
- "Most of our project work is through repeat business or recommendations from people we work with."
- "There are 6 partners… all involved and focused on project delivery."
- "Our flat company structure means that we do not work in isolated, internal teams thereby allowing us flexibility to allocate staff as required."
- "We believe in delivering sustainable and efficient designs in order to create the highest quality buildings and internal environments."
- "Our delivery approach tests and validates design, enabling appropriate measures to be identified early in the design process."
- "We are committed to providing the best-suited people to each project and we always aim to retain the same team members from start to finish."
- "Scotch is wholly owned and run by the Partners and we are trading profitably without any borrowings."

**Section 3 — team framing:**
- "We propose to use the same team members that supported the acquisition stage of the project as this team is known to [Client]."
- "The team are all based in our London office in Farringdon."
- "In order to establish clear lines of communication between the client and project team we have proposed to structure ourselves in a way to delineate reporting and communication with wider stakeholders."

**Section 4 — fees framing:**
- "Our overall MEP and Energy fees can be summarised in the table below."
- "Optional fees for Acoustics can be found below:-"
- "Optional fees for BREEAM can be found below:-"
- Standard clarifications bullets — VAT, expenses, programme, planning, Stage 4i by contractor, client monitoring, no novation, EPC lodging, energy methodology, acoustic testing, vibration etc.
- "Our rates for management, design and advisory activities are as follows (note these rates will differ from our rates used to calculate the fee – this is not a mistake)."

**Appendix A — scope language:**
- Stage 2 / 3+ bullet lists are highly standardised and re-usable.
