## 2. Scope type

Refurbishment / reconfiguration of an existing locally listed building. **Not a new build, not a fit-out** — material internal reconfiguration plus selective fabric and plant interventions. Town Legal advise the principal reconfiguration works do **not** require planning permission; lifts, ASHPs and associated plant **may** require planning, subject to final design. CIL liability "unlikely" given no significant net floorspace gain.

## 3. Proposed scheme (from the RFP)

The existing building will be "reconfigured to optimise space and functionality" through:
- (a) 93 ensuite studio units averaging 17 sq m, the majority with mezzanines adding 3.75 sq m → 19.2 sq m average usable;
- (b) Two new lifts, both meeting fire evacuation standards;
- (c) Introduction of Air Source Heat Pumps;
- (d) Shared amenity space totalling 91 sq m;
- (e) Targeting EPC B;
- (f) A high-spec fit-out to help differentiate the product.

Additional optional element noted in the planning note: a possible roof terrace on the northern-most block, using the existing stair access; would attract overlooking / noise / hours-of-use considerations from LBS.

## 4. Services in scope (what Scotch's MEP/SEP/Acoustics response should cover)

The RFP devotes most of its body text to MEP-related "key considerations" — i.e. the issues Scotch's response will need to engage with. In summary:

- **Heating / DHW** — replace existing gas system with ASHPs on each block's roof (subject to feasibility). Decentralised DHW with dedicated pump rooms and calorifiers per block.
- **Fabric / windows** — new high-performance double-pane windows required; further fabric upgrades possible.
- **Energy / EPC** — uplift from EPC E to EPC B. (The Scotch-issued 31/01/2025 report cited in the RFP underpins this.) Note: Part O does not apply (not a new build).
- **Electrical** — main incoming supply at end of serviceable life per Fulkers Bailey Russell — to be replaced. Power load expected to increase given electrification; full assessment / upgrade allowance required. Existing LPN substation chamber retained.
- **Public health — cold water** — central cold water storage tank and pump set under consideration; flow/pressure tests required.
- **Public health — drainage** — CCTV survey needed of below-ground; additional drainage surveys to confirm existing connections and approach to new plumbing stacks. Above-ground drainage in scope per Scotch's example.
- **Fire** — sprinklers throughout (residential >11 m); evacuation lifts with backup power; dry risers possibly per stair core. Fire engineer engaged (Ben Green of Semper); strategy "approved by Ben Green of Semper".
- **Lifts** — emergency generator backup requirement to be defined.
- **Acoustics** — full acoustic assessment required for ASHP rooftop locations; potential roof terrace would carry hours-of-use / noise reporting implications.
- **Asbestos** — 2014 survey by Phil Brown of eSafe found no risk in accessed areas; intrusive survey required before refurb / demo works.
- **Structure (not Scotch scope)** — Frame Engineers reviewed 15/01/2025, condition "reasonable"; rooftop plant loading and waterproofing to be confirmed.
- **Sustainability** — EPC B target dominates; no explicit BREEAM target named in this RFP.

## 7. Specific constraints

- **Heritage / planning** — Locally Listed; heritage advice on façade still recommended despite no listed-building consent requirement. Roof-level plant and lifts may attract planning attention.
- **Trees** — TPO group at front; pruning/removal would require approval.
- **No basement excavation** — explicit RFP constraint due to dense utilities under the courtyard and existing footprint.
- **Substation easement** — LPN substation chamber leased since 1991 must be retained; constrains ground-floor layout.
- **EPC** — target EPC B (uplift from current EPC E).
- **Fire** — building >11 m height drives sprinklering throughout; evacuation lifts with backup power; possible dry risers per stair core; Ben Green of Semper retained as fire consultant.
- **Acoustics** — rooftop ASHP locations and (optional) roof terrace require formal acoustic assessment.
- **Occupied works** — not occupied (vacant since March 2020).
- **No planning consents on file at Southwark** — increases due-diligence load on the design team.
- **Budget envelope** — not stated in the RFP.

## 8. What's being asked of Scotch

The RFP says: "LG Living Ltd (LGL) has prepared this document to support your fee proposal for the redevelopment of 83 New Kent Rd" and "You are expecting to provide a fee proposal from RIBA Stage 2 – 5." Mapping to the example's response shape:

- Fee proposal covering MEP and Energy across **RIBA Stage 2–5** (the example also offers Stage 6 commissioning review under MEP).
- The example offered Acoustics and BREEAM as **optional** strands — the new RFP does not name BREEAM but explicitly calls for "a full acoustic assessment". So Acoustics should be included; BREEAM can stay optional, framed as available if required.
- Scotch-specific positioning: Scotch were already engaged at acquisition / due-diligence stage (the RFP cites the Scotch report dated 31/01/2025 on EPC uplift). So "continue with the same team" is a credible framing.
- No specific MEP scope of works is supplied with the RFP — so the response must include a scope of services appendix (as the example does).
- The RFP does not pose specific narrative questions (no Q&A format) — it expects a structured fee proposal with assumptions, clarifications and a scope appendix.
