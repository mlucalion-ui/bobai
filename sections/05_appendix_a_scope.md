# Appendix A — MEP and Energy Scope of Services

## MEP Scope of Services

### Briefing — Stage 1
Undertaken already as part of the acquisition stage of the scheme; the Scotch report dated 31/01/2025 sets out the recommended MEP interventions and EPC uplift route.

### Concept Design — Stage 2
Deliverables:
- Building services issues report as part of the concept design report, including a desk study on matters affecting design options:
  - recommendations for renewables (including ASHP location feasibility for each block roof)
  - lift MEP interfaces (lift design excluded)
  - environmental assessments
  - Building Control requirements
  - noise and acoustic measures (MEP plant only)
  - fire and smoke control measures in respect of MEP services
  - adequacy of utilities supplies (incoming electrical, water, drainage)
  - constraints arising from the brief or local authority policy
  - research on innovative solutions
- Concept design model with appropriate geometric detail and object information
- Concept sketch drawings for the preferred preliminary design(s)
- Concept schematics for the preferred preliminary design(s)
- Information for early-stage life-cycle cost and life-cycle assessment studies
- Cost-plan support for building services based on floor area, building type and system assumptions
- High-level metering strategy

### Scheme Design — Stage 3 Plus (tender documentation)
Deliverables:
- Work alongside any PCSA-appointed contractor in developing additional packages
- Identify long-lead items with the contractor and develop the design to a level at which procurement could occur (e.g. AHUs, generators)
- Updated building services issues report at developed-design stage, including a desk study on:
  - renewables recommendations
  - considerations for off-site manufacture
  - environmental assessments
  - Building Control requirements
  - noise and acoustic measures, as advised by the project acoustic consultant
  - fire and smoke control measures in respect of MEP services
  - adequacy of utilities supplies
- Updated energy statement for planning submission, where required, based on the agreed energy strategy
- Updated design risk assessments
- Performance tender specifications and/or main plant equipment schedules for MEP services
- Developed design (BSRIA 3a) model with appropriate geometric detail and object information
- Developed design (BSRIA 3a) sketch drawings and schematics for the preferred design(s)
- Updated information for life-cycle cost and life-cycle assessment studies
- Developed design input into the (BSRIA 3a) cost plan for building services
- Updated metering strategy
- Provision of Employer's Requirements and associated tender documents (if required)

### Detailed Design — Stage 4 i, ii, iii
Scotch to undertake a client monitoring role on the final MEP design — one iteration of comments and a final check that the contractor has addressed those comments.

### Construction — Stage 5
- Sample review and comment on the Contractor's installation drawings and technical submissions against the agreed Employer's Requirements, Client Proposals and User Requirements Specification. We will review circa 100% of construction-stage MEP information, once.
- Raise observations and any areas of non-compliance to the Clerk of Works.
- Monitor the Contractor's progress against the agreed construction programme.
- Provide site observations and progress reports on a monthly basis.

### Construction — Stage 6
- Review of commissioning documentation and sign-offs against the Employer's Requirements.
- Sample witnessing of MEP systems (typically 10%).
- Review of and comment on O&M Manuals.
- MEP status report at practical completion.
- Attendance at the practical completion meeting.

## Acoustics Scope of Services

### RIBA Stage 2 — Planning / Concept Support
- Gain an understanding of the scheme proposals to date and any limitations or constraints imposed on the site. Consolidate the design information produced to date and any brand standards, quality and sustainability targets that are to be adopted.
- Identify any additional noise / vibration survey works required for design and / or planning-related works, including the rooftop ASHP locations referenced in the RFP.
- Establish any acoustic constraints likely to be imposed by Southwark or other stakeholders, including limits to noise emission from building services plant and external noise intrusion. Conclude this with a report suitable for submission in support of any planning application that may arise from the rooftop plant or possible roof terrace.
- Attend meetings and workshops with the design team to help develop the acoustic concept. Conclude with a report suitable for inclusion within the Stage 2 documentation.
- Establish appropriate acoustic performance specifications for background noise levels, building services noise levels, internal sound insulation, external noise intrusion and room-acoustic performance. This will take account of good-practice guidance, BREEAM (if pursued), the Building Regulations and any client-specific requirements.

### RIBA Stage 3 — Developed Design
- Assist the relevant engineers with development or review of:
  - ventilation strategies
  - façade design options
  - suitable partitions and floors
  - room geometry and finishes
  - environmental noise control
  - plant selection and building services noise control
- Undertake any additional noise / vibration survey works required during the design stage.
- Provide sufficient design-stage evidence to achieve any targeted acoustic credits under BREEAM, WELL etc. as necessary.
- Attend design meetings and workshops as required.
- Provide notes and sketches as required.
- Provide acoustic mark-up drawings showing performance criteria.
- Provide a report or reports containing RIBA Stage 3 commentary on:
  - performance criteria for acceptable internal ambient noise levels
  - external noise levels
  - required glazing and ventilation treatments
  - performance criteria for separating partitions, floors and roof
  - performance criteria for building services design
  - performance criteria for reverberation control
  - development and review of partition, floor and roof constructions
  - absorptive treatments for reverberation control
  - mitigation measures for external plant noise (including the rooftop ASHPs)
  - BREEAM / WELL compliance as necessary

### RIBA Stage 4 — Technical Design
Acoustics monitoring role only — one iteration of comments on contractor documents.

### RIBA Stages 5 and 6
- Attend tender-stage meetings as necessary.
- Review technical proposals for compliance with the tender requirements.
- Contribute to the tender evaluation report as required.
- Provide ongoing assistance to the team as reasonably required.
- Provide a Client-side advisor role throughout construction.
- Review formal Contractor submissions that require Client sign-off in respect of acoustics.
- Review alternative proposals from the Contractor, communicating issues clearly where Client guidance is required.
- Undertake regular site inspections and report findings; provide guidance where relevant.
- Review and agree acoustic testing proposals (expected to be carried out by the Contractor or a third party on their behalf) for the purpose of demonstrating compliance with the acoustic requirements.
- Review acoustic test results and provide commentary to the Client-side team.
- Conclude with a formal sign-off document once the acoustic requirements have been demonstrably met.
