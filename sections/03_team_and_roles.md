# 3. Team and Proposed Roles

## 3.1 Organogram

[Organogram diagram — image asset to be inserted in production version. Shows LGL (Harry Keen) at the top, the Scotch core leadership team (John Steele lead; Mark Bacon, Kirsten Elder, Jason Clouston) reporting in, Raphael Boothman as MEP co-ordinator, and the wider design team as recipients of co-ordination.]

We propose to use the same team members who supported the acquisition stage of the project, as this team is already known to LGL and was responsible for the 31st January 2025 EPC uplift report cited in your RFP. The team are all based in our London office in Farringdon.

In order to establish clear lines of communication between the client and the project team, we have proposed to structure ourselves in a way that delineates reporting and communication with wider stakeholders.

The core leadership team will be led by John Steele, who will be the overall point of contact for day-to-day matters. John will be supported by Mark Bacon (leading on utilities and the electrical Peer Review), with Kirsten Elder and Jason Clouston picking up sustainability and acoustics respectively, should we be appointed for those strands. This provides a clear leadership structure for reporting and feedback, and allows existing relationships with the client team to develop further.

A similar approach is needed to communicate with the wider team. We therefore propose Raphael Boothman as the Scotch MEP co-ordinator. Raphael's role is to liaise with the wider design team to ensure that information flows smoothly and co-ordination matters are dealt with swiftly and pro-actively.
