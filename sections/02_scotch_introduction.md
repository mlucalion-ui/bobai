# 2. Scotch Partners Introduction

Scotch have a proven track record working with workplace and science and technology clients and are currently working on a number of similar residential and co-living schemes, including refurbishment projects comparable in scale and character to 83 New Kent Road. Scotch are a building design practice providing mechanical, electrical & public health engineering, sustainability, energy and planning (SEP) and acoustic consultancy services.

The culture of the practice is founded upon respect for people and trust. We are committed to creating a great and inclusive working environment where the best minds in the industry will flourish. This is supported by our high retention rate, which was c.97% for recently completed FY 24.

Since its inception, Scotch has organically grown, being careful to employ people who believe in our shared values of quality, collaboration and trust. With a team of circa 65 people, our size and ownership enable us to have total control over workload and resource, working on small, medium and large-scale projects. Most of our project work comes through repeat business or recommendations from people we work with — testament to the team and the work we produce, which is founded on quality and attention to detail.

We work on a wide variety of projects in relation to the type and scale of this project, with a high proportion of our work within the workplace and science and technology sectors.

## Management Structure

Scotch operates as one business unit / team, reflected by the flat and simple management structure. There are 6 partners — with John Steele having recently joined as a Partner — all involved in and focused on project delivery. Reporting into the Partners and helping deliver the projects are the Technical Directors, including a Business Operations Director.

Our flat company structure means that we do not work in isolated, internal teams, allowing us flexibility to allocate staff as required. This also supports sharing of knowledge across the business. As the company is wholly owned by the Partners, we have complete control over our operation and resource.

## Quality & Delivery

We believe in delivering sustainable and efficient designs in order to create the highest quality buildings and internal environments. To do so, we are collaborative and client-focused, providing considerable combined experience in building services design engineering, modelling, energy, sustainability, wellness and acoustic consultancy services. This philosophy is integral to our service.

We understand the importance of clarity and knowledge in the decision-making process. Our delivery approach tests and validates design, enabling appropriate measures to be identified early in the design process. This avoids the need for inefficient re-testing of solutions.

We are committed to providing the best-suited people to each project and we always aim to retain the same team members from start to finish. We have a robust approach to covering absences of key personnel that ensures uninterrupted support and project continuity.

## Financial Standing & Ownership

Scotch is wholly owned and run by the Partners and we are trading profitably without any borrowings.
