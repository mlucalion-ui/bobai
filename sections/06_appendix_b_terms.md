# Appendix B — Scotch Partners Terms & Conditions

> **Note for the prototype:** In production, this appendix should pull Scotch's current standard appointment terms from a fixed template file rather than be regenerated. The 16-clause structure below is preserved from the example response (R02) and summarises the substance of each clause; for the issued draft, replace this section with the firm's standard terms.

By instructing Scotch Partners LLP to commence the Services, you accept the appointment on the following terms, which will apply for the duration of the appointment unless we agree alternative terms in writing.

1. **Standard of care.** We will exercise reasonable skill, care and diligence in performing the Services and meeting any agreed programme, subject to receipt of necessary briefing, instructions and information. If you request a variation, we will notify you as soon as reasonably practicable of the impact on cost and programme.

2. **Invoicing.** Unless otherwise agreed in writing, we will invoice monthly in arrears for Services performed in the preceding calendar month, and on termination or completion. The due date for payment is two (2) days after issue; the final date is twenty-eight (28) days after the due date.

3. **Late payment.** If invoices remain unpaid after the final date, we may (a) charge interest under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998 together with reasonable costs of recovery, and / or (b) suspend the Services until arrears and interest are paid in cleared funds, without prejudice to Clause 6.

4. **Professional indemnity insurance.** We will maintain PI insurance at no less than the level set out in Clause 5, provided such insurance remains available on commercially reasonable terms.

5. **Liability cap.** Our total liability in connection with this appointment, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), breach of statutory duty or otherwise, will not exceed ten (10) times our aggregate fee for the Services. Nothing in this appointment limits liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence or for fraudulent misrepresentation.

6. **Net contribution.** Without prejudice to Clause 5, our liability is further limited to the share that it would be just and equitable for us to pay, assuming other consultants, contractors and sub-contractors with concurrent liability have given undertakings on terms no less onerous than Clause 1 and have paid their just-and-equitable share.

7. **Indemnity over the cap.** If claims result in our paying amounts in excess of the Clause 5 cap, you will indemnify us in respect of all amounts above the cap.

8. **No claims against individuals.** Save for death or personal injury, you will look only to Scotch Partners LLP (and not to any individual employee) for redress, and agree not to pursue claims against individual employees arising from their performance of the Services.

9. **Intellectual property.** Copyright in all drawings, designs, documents and materials we prepare for you remains vested in us, subject to a licence to you to use them for the purposes for which they were prepared, provided you have paid our fees in full. We are not liable for use of our intellectual property for any other purpose.

10. **Termination — insolvency.** Either party may terminate immediately by written notice if the other becomes insolvent, has a bankruptcy order made against it, makes an arrangement with creditors, enters liquidation, or has insolvency proceedings commenced against it.

11. **Termination — default.** Either party may terminate by written notice if the other substantially fails to perform its obligations, provided the terminating party has given fourteen (14) days' written notice (by recorded delivery) specifying the default with reference to this clause, and the default has not been remedied in that period.

12. **Termination payment.** On termination for any cause, we are entitled to be paid for all Services performed up to the date of termination and not yet paid for.

13. **Personal appointment; third-party rights.** This appointment is personal to you and non-assignable. It does not confer rights on any third party under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 or otherwise. Collateral warranties to third parties are excluded.

14. **Dispute resolution; governing law.** Any dispute arising under this appointment that cannot be settled amicably may be referred by either party to adjudication under the Construction Industry Council Model Adjudication Procedure (3rd Edition). The parties will agree a sole adjudicator, failing which the CIC will nominate one. The adjudication will be conducted in English under the laws of England and Wales. This appointment is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

15. **Entire agreement.** This appointment represents the entire agreement and understanding between the parties relating to its subject matter. You acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to negotiate its terms.

16. **Bespoke contracts and warranties.** Bespoke contracts and collateral warranties are excluded on projects below a minimum fee value of £10,000. Review and completion of such documents will be charged at Partner fee rates, and legal fees may apply.
