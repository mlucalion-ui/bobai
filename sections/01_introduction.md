# 1. Introduction

Scotch Partners are delighted to have been invited by Harry Keen of LGL to provide a fee and scope for the design of the 83 New Kent Road redevelopment. This is a great project and we are pleased to continue from the acquisition stage of the scheme, where our report dated 31st January 2025 set out the recommended MEP interventions to uplift the building from EPC E to EPC B.

We understand that the scheme is close to exchange and completion (programmed for 20th February 2025) and that LGL are keen to receive proposals so that the design team can hit the ground running. Scotch Partners propose to keep the same team on the project that supported the acquisition. To summarise, the scheme entails the following:

- The site comprises three purpose-built interconnected buildings of between 2 and 4 storeys around a central courtyard, totalling 26,615 sq ft (2,473 sq m) GIA, currently vacant student accommodation last occupied by London South Bank University.
- The existing building will be reconfigured to optimise space and functionality, delivering 93 ensuite studio units averaging 17 sq m, with mezzanines on the majority bringing the average usable space to 19.2 sq m.
- Two new lifts, both meeting fire evacuation standards, equipped with backup power.
- Introduction of Air Source Heat Pumps on each block's roof, replacing the existing gas system for heating and domestic hot water.
- Shared amenity space totalling 91 sq m.
- Targeting EPC B (uplift from current EPC E).
- A high-spec fit-out to help differentiate the product.
- It is anticipated that the principal reconfiguration works will not require a planning application, per the Maddox Planning note dated 11/02/2025; lifts, ASHPs and associated plant may attract an application subject to final design.
- The development is to be treated as 'non domestic' for energy / EPC purposes. Part O does not apply as the works are to an existing building rather than new-build residential.
- High level programme: Stage 2 by 14th May 2025; Stage 3+ sign-off by 15th October 2025; main works February 2026 to February 2027; target completion 16th June 2027 (28 months from exchange).
- Fees have been provided for MEP and Sustainability / Energy, with optional strands for Acoustics and BREEAM.
