## Clarifications

- Fees quoted exclude VAT, which will be charged at the prevailing rate.
- Fees include normal expenses; as the project is in Elephant and Castle, we expect this to be minimal.
- MEP fees are based on an anticipated design programme of 10 weeks for Stage 2 (to 14/05/25), 19 weeks for Stage 3+ (to 15/10/25), and a construction stage of 52 weeks of main works (05/02/26 → 17/02/27) plus 13 weeks of programme contingency to target completion on 16/06/27.
- Assumes no planning deliverables. The principal reconfiguration works do not require a planning application per the Maddox Planning note dated 11/02/2025, save for any application arising from lifts, ASHPs or associated rooftop plant; an updated CIL position is also assumed unlikely.
- Assumes Stage 4i design undertaken by the MEP contractor. Scotch Partners are able to undertake the 4ii/iii design role if required by the sub-contractor.
- Client monitoring role only across Stages 4–6 (no novation allowed for).
- No MEP scope of works was provided as part of the RFP. We have therefore provided a scope of works as an appendix to this document.
- We have allowed for above-ground drainage. We have not allowed for below-ground or slab drainage (including pumping etc.), as we assume this will form part of the civil and structural engineers' scope. The CCTV survey of existing below-ground drainage referenced in the RFP "Next Steps" is assumed to be commissioned separately by LGL.
- Cold-water flow and pressure tests referenced in the RFP "Next Steps" are assumed to be commissioned separately by LGL.
- Fire engineering excluded. We understand that Ben Green of Semper is retained as fire consultant; our MEP design will co-ordinate with the Semper-approved strategy (sprinklering throughout for the >11 m residential application, evacuation lifts with backup power, and dry risers per stair core if required).
- Lift design excluded. We will provide MEP interfaces only, including definition of the emergency generator backup requirement for the evacuation lifts in conjunction with the appointed lift specialist.
- Energy clarification — we have included 5 no. EPC options tests and 3 no. thermal comfort options at Stage 2. Stage 3+ fees assume a fixed design.
- Fees are based on the EPC methodology at the time of writing. EPC and Part L methodology are under consultation by the government and may be updated during the construction period.
- We have not included for a lodged EPC at the end of construction. This is assumed to be the contractor's responsibility.
- We have not included for an Energy Statement at planning, as this is understood not to be required.
- Scheme assumed to be 'non domestic' as per guidance from the project planning consultant and client. Part O (overheating in dwellings) does not apply, as the works are to an existing Sui Generis building rather than a new-build residential development.
- No on-site acoustic testing — to be undertaken by the contractor — review only by Scotch.
- Vibration excluded.
- Intrusive asbestos survey assumed to be commissioned separately by LGL ahead of any soft-strip works, per the RFP "Next Steps"; review only by Scotch.
- Substation chamber on the ground floor (leased to London Power Networks PLC since 1991) assumed to be retained in its current form; no allowance has been made for substation works or re-provision.
